# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security vulnerability in Baron Pit, please report it privately
by email to **security@baronpit.com**. Do not open a public issue or discuss it
publicly before it has been addressed.

Please include:

- A description of the vulnerability and what it allows.
- The affected page, feature or URL.
- Steps to reproduce, with the smallest proof of concept that demonstrates it.
- The impact as you understand it, and a way to reach you for follow-up.

We read every report sent to that address. The same contact is published in
machine-readable form at
[baronpit.com/.well-known/security.txt](https://baronpit.com/.well-known/security.txt).

## Community safety reports are different

**safety@baronpit.com** is for community-safety reports — harassment, threats,
child safety and similar conduct issues. It is not the channel for technical
vulnerabilities; please use **security@baronpit.com** for those so they reach
the right handling path.
