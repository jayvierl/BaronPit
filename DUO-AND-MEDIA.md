# Verified duo matching and media operations

## Duo Finder

Public lookups and email accounts work independently of Riot Sign On. An account's self-claimed link cannot publish or view a verified listing. The account data model continues to label those claims as unverified.

Leave `DUO_MATCHING_ENABLED` unset until Riot has approved RSO, an approved linking flow verifies the Riot subject, and staffed safety coverage is in place. The approved client uses the Client Secret Basic flow. Register the exact HTTPS callback at /api/account/riot/callback, then configure RIOT_RSO_CLIENT_ID, RIOT_RSO_CLIENT_SECRET and RIOT_RSO_REDIRECT_URI in that environment. The callback asks Riot for the signed-in account, matches its PUUID against the selected profile, and serializes competing verifications. Never set verification on a player claim by hand. Use team-controlled test identities and check role/rank, unlinking, expired snapshots, blocking and the reporter inbox before any public activation. We store neither Riot access tokens nor refresh tokens. A live round trip still requires approved credentials and a consenting test player.

With those prerequisites in place, Duo Finder checks current terms, the same private age band, the same game platform, a minimum account age of 24 hours and a Riot ranked snapshot with recent games fetched within the previous day. A player can close a listing without creating a new one. Guests and ineligible accounts get a useful status page. A match requires a request from one player and an explicit acceptance by the other before either Riot ID is returned. First-contact access ends when a listing expires. Unlinking, blocking, muting or losing eligibility also removes contact. After a match, each player can privately choose to play together again. It requires the participants’ current Riot snapshots to agree on a shared game after acceptance and identify them as teammates. If both opt in, the pair can see the other player’s Riot ID for up to 30 days after acceptance. Either player can withdraw. The service stores two choices and an acceptance timestamp. Raw games remain in the usual replaceable recent-match snapshot.

Listings run four hours. A recent, unreported pairing can support an endorsement or report for 30 days after expiry. The daily authenticated maintenance job removes up to 500 old new-format listings per run, with their requests. Listings with holds, decisions or reports, and records from the earlier site, are excluded. Operators must separately resolve and retain case evidence under the approved retention schedule. New helpful-builder endorsements require an actual reply to the giver’s post. Teammate endorsements require an accepted duo plus a matching recent Riot game with same-team identification. Counts use interaction-checked endorsements in the last 90 days. A player may endorse three people per day and the same person once per 30 days.

## Private post-game thanks

Either participant can send one free, fixed thank-you once a shared same-team game after the accepted request is present in both current Riot snapshots. The other player sees it in that request. Sending it never consents to another game or reveals a Riot ID. Blocks, mutes, lost eligibility and active safety reports prevent new thanks. The two flags expire with the listing record and carry no score, cash amount, or public badge.

The example at `/lfg/thanks` is a local calculator in USD. Its illustrative platform share defaults to 10%. Operators may set `DUO_TIP_PREVIEW_BPS` to a whole number of basis points between 0 and 10000 to compare pricing assumptions. The platform share is rounded to the nearest cent, and the two shares sum to the example amount. Provider fees and taxes are outside this illustration. Users enter no payment or payout details. Approval for payment processing and the specific revenue model is a separate decision from Riot Sign On or a production API key.

## Private duo preferences

Players can privately save their role, preferred partner role, broad play times, vibe and desired chat preference. This works while verified matching is paused. A plan creates no listing or invitation. Guests can explore the controls and keep a short-lived browser draft through sign-in. A signed-in player can clear the plan in Duo Finder. Account deletion cascades to the preference row.

When verified matching is enabled, the board applies these preferences inside the same age, server, Riot verification, block and mute boundaries as the ordinary listings. The filtered view matches the requested role (including Fill), at least one selected local time label, compatible playstyle and the requested communication setting. Local time labels do not establish a common time zone. Players can switch to all eligible listings. A new listing starts from the owner's role, vibe and usual times. Opening voice chat always requires an explicit choice.

## Existing Blob assets

This release disables the public Blob token issuer, suppresses historical uploaded media and declines direct media posts. **A previously shared public Blob URL may still work outside the application.** Changing the UI cannot revoke a public URL.

Run `node scripts/media-inventory.mjs --community` within the existing project environment to count store objects, total bytes, legacy media posts and posts under review. The command reads listing metadata only and never prints object names or URLs. A missing provider or database connection is reported as unverified. The operator should inventory previous public objects through the authenticated Vercel project, check for legal obligations and active safety reports, and document the disposition of each object. Keep suspected evidence under designated private custody with controlled access. Obtain approval for irreversible deletion or changes to the existing store. Never paste original media, previously public URLs, sensitive report text or credentials into the moderation queue, a staging page or operator chat. Inform affected users of any material change in handling.

New clip-link posts allow canonical video pages from supported providers. Each starts held, carries a server-controlled marker, and requires human approval. Approved links open the publisher’s site when clicked. Historical clip links have no review marker and are excluded. Ordinary viewers never need the provider's player, thumbnails or Blob SDK to read a thread.

Direct photo and video uploads need a private quarantine store, scanner integration covering known-abuse hashes, decoded visuals, OCR and speech, authenticated results bound to exact content, protected evidence retention, and trained primary and backup responders. These services and registrations are external release requirements. Configure none by adding secrets to source control. An available upload token or a successful encoding operation cannot establish safety clearance.
