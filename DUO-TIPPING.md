# Post-game duo appreciation

The proposal is a voluntary thank-you after two players accept a pairing and play on the same team. Matching, sending thanks and opting to play again should stay free. A player’s spending, receipt of tips or decision to skip should never influence who sees their listing, their rank, or their partner’s ability to contact them.

## Cash eligibility

**Riot.** The League developer policy requires an Approved or Acknowledged registered product, a free tier, and transformative content when charging for content. It lists donations as an acceptable way a product may charge and directs developers to ask about uncertain models. The documented application is pending. The policy gives no express answer for passing a cash tip to a ranked teammate while retaining a platform fee. Jay can attach the post-game screens and fee example to application 911877 and ask about this particular use. Riot lists LFG as a production-key use case. Its rank-manipulation guidance covers account sharing and intentionally deranking to raise another player’s rank. The proposed design excludes paid access to partners, offers before a game, carries, and rewards conditional on wins, LP, or performance statistics. [League developer policy](https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol) · [Riot rank-manipulation guidance](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/penalties/mmr-elo-boosting-rank-manipulation)

**Payment provider.** Stripe permits tips for a good or service already provided and expressly excludes personal transfers between individuals. Two people playing a game together provides evidence of contact. It does not establish a qualifying commercial service. Taking a commission, checking both players’ identities or calling the transfer a donation does not resolve that distinction. Before building live collection, give a candidate processor the exact money flow, countries, ages, purpose and percentage. Obtain its eligibility determination for that activity. If it classifies the transfer as prohibited, keep cash off with that processor. [Stripe tip requirements](https://support.stripe.com/questions/requirements-for-accepting-tips-or-donations) · [Restricted businesses](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses)

**PayPal as a second candidate.** PayPal Multiparty supports commissions on transactions processed on behalf of an onboarded seller. Live access requires platform approval. Its public partner documentation does not identify an ordinary teammate receiving an unsolicited gift as an eligible seller. Ask PayPal's platforms team to classify this exact activity before selecting an integration. [PayPal marketplace overview and partner application](https://developer.paypal.com/platforms/overview/)

**Future native apps.** Apple’s optional personal-gift exception and Google Play’s direct creator-tip exception both require the recipient to receive the entire amount. A platform commission falls outside these particular exceptions. Review applicable regional billing programs if a native app is submitted. The present product is a website. [App Review Guidelines, §3.2.1(vii)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#acceptable) · [Google Play payments FAQ](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10281818?hl=en)

## How an eligible split would work

Keep any preview conspicuously illustrative. Jay chooses the business rate and the payer of processing fees after receiving actual provider terms. Show the payer’s total, the platform’s fee and the partner’s share before consent. Calculate the fee once in integer minor units and allocate the remainder to the partner. A platform fee is gross revenue. The amount left after processing fees, refunds and disputes determines the margin.

If Stripe accepts the exact model, Connect destination charges can collect a server-set application fee. The platform balance is liable for processing fees, refunds and chargebacks. Use provider-hosted recipient onboarding, verify payout eligibility, reconcile signed and deduplicated payment events, and test partial refunds, transfer reversals, fee refunds and disputes before moving money. A checkout success page alone cannot establish settlement. The platform would also need to confirm supported countries, age eligibility, financial-record retention, tax reporting responsibilities and a staffed refund and dispute process for its chosen configuration. [Stripe destination charges](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/destination-charges) · [Hosted onboarding](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/hosted-onboarding)

## Evidence for the provider and Riot

Show the full path from free discovery through mutual acceptance, verified same-team play, appreciation and the optional fee illustration. Include the block/report path, the result-independent eligibility rule, restrictions on payment solicitation, and the way a player skips. Record each separate provider and Riot decision, including which countries and payment flow it covers. A production API key or working payment sandbox alone does not answer the business-model question.

**For Riot, in the notes for application 911877**

> We are building a free League duo finder. After two adult players accept a pairing and complete a verified game on the same team, either could privately offer an optional cash tip. The platform would retain a disclosed percentage. Players pay nothing to match or play again, and the tip is available after wins and losses. There are no promised carries, paid listings or rank rewards. Does Riot permit this specific flow, including the commission, and what conditions or registration changes would you require before release?

**For a payment provider’s platform review team**

> We are evaluating a US web pilot for adults. A player could voluntarily tip an ordinary teammate after a verified League of Legends match. There is no underlying purchase, coaching service or promised game result. We would display and retain a percentage of each tip and route the balance to the teammate. Does your platform support this precise activity? If so, which program and transaction classification apply, which recipients may onboard, and who bears processing, refund and dispute costs?
